## Risks

**Location is the dominant risk, and it is not a resume problem.** The role is five days a week in a Mumbai office with remote explicitly ruled out. Candidate is based in Delhi NCR. This is a genuine relocation, not a formality, and it will be the first filter applied.

It also runs against the candidate's current live negotiation: the Foldr Studio thread concluded on 2026-07-20 at a 20% hike for a **remote** role, with a first interview held 2026-07-27. If remote is the actual preference, this application conflicts with that and should be a deliberate choice rather than a reflex.

**Seniority framing.** JD asks for 3+ years; candidate has 6+. Less acute than the Classcard 0-2 year gap, and 6+ sits comfortably above a 3+ floor rather than overshooting it. No mitigation applied beyond keeping the tone execution-focused.

**Company entity ambiguity.** The post came from `regenesys.net` and the JD names "Digital Regenesys". Regenesys Education is South Africa-headquartered with India operations; the public site does not list a Mumbai office. The Mumbai location comes from the LinkedIn post and the JD, not from independent confirmation. Worth verifying the office address before any interview travel.

**Recruiter contact is a personal-name address** (`kundann@regenesys.net`) on the company domain, which is normal and unlike the Harshita Hiring Desk pattern flagged separately. No scam markers here: real company domain, real JD document, named role, stated location.
