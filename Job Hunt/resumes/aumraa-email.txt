2. AI-Assisted Projects & How I Refined Them

• Maximor AI — AI Finance Platform Landing & Visual Assets (https://www.maximor.ai/cfo-offer-all)
  - The Idea: Translate complex AI close/forecasting workflows into high-clarity visual metaphors for finance leaders.
  - Tools Used: Midjourney + ChatGPT + Figma.
  - Refinement & Design Judgment: Raw AI prompts produced overly sci-fi, generic circuit-board clichés. I constrained prompts to architectural lighting and tactile textures, brought the outputs into Figma and Photoshop to rebuild lighting curves, integrated custom typography hierarchies, and ensured every asset served the product narrative.

• La Well & Herbal Hand — E-commerce & Performance Creatives (200+ Assets, 10L+ Views)
  - The Idea: Build high-volume D2C catalog imagery and performance ad creatives across a 35-SKU herbal wellness line.
  - Tools Used: Midjourney + Recraft + Adobe Photoshop.
  - Refinement & Design Judgment: Used AI to generate varied contextual lifestyle environments and lighting setups. Every product bottle, label, typography layer, and shadow interaction was manually composited and color-graded in Photoshop to eliminate AI warping, maintain exact packaging fidelity, and ensure natural, culturally grounded aesthetics.

• Mymy & Brand Visual Systems — AI-Explored Brand Concepts & Packaging Renders
  - The Idea: Rapidly test distinct visual identities, bottle silhouettes, and mood directions for boutique consumer brands before locking the final design language.
  - Tools Used: Midjourney + Claude + Illustrator / Photoshop.
  - Refinement & Design Judgment: Claude helped structure thematic concept variations; Midjourney generated rapid mood boards and material studies. From there, I stripped away non-functional decorative artifacts, rebuilt clean vector logos and grid systems in Illustrator, and finalized packaging mockups with realistic material depth.
